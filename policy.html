Evidence into policy. Policy into delivery.

Governments do not lack intent — they lack bandwidth, evidence and follow-through. The practice embeds with state institutions and think tanks to supply all three: rigorous research, implementable program design, and the monitoring that keeps a scheme honest.

From white paper to field.
